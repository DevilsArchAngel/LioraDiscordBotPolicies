# Liora's Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 4 November 2026*

Hi! This is how Liora Altima, the Discord companion, looks after your information. We've kept it in plain words.
The short version: **Liora keeps as little as she can, for as short as she can, never sells anything, and you can
see or erase what she has about you at any time.**

Liora is made and run by **Bliss** (BlissfulWizard on Discord) with a small team of friends. She's a hobby
project in a small closed beta, not a company.

---

## 1. Your two magic commands

- **`/privacy mydata`** privately shows you everything Liora keeps about you, in every server.
- **`/privacy forgetme`** erases it. A few things stay on purpose, like moderation records (see section 6).

You never need to ask anyone for permission to use them.

(come back sooner and it's all still there). Things that belong to *you* rather than a server, like playlists,
cosmetics and birthdays, go once you've been gone from **every** server Liora is in for 72 hours.

**What she never keeps:** confessions, quote cards, Mafia games, mini-games, invite logs, or your login. Those
either aren't saved at all or are ordinary Discord messages in a server, which that server's staff control.

## 3. Who else sees your information

**Liora never sells or rents anything about you, and never shows you ads.** To work, she uses a few outside
services. Each gets only what it needs:

| Service | What it gets | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Discord** | Everything Liora does happens on Discord | It's where she lives |
| **Google Gemini** | The conversation she's replying to; server details when writing a Library blurb or weekly recap | To think of her replies |
| **Anthropic Claude** | The same, but only when Claude is answering instead of Gemini (Gemini's credit has run out, Gemini can't be reached, or Bliss chose Claude) | To think of her replies |
| **Giphy, Pexels, DuckDuckGo** | Search words only, never your messages | To find GIFs and pictures |
| **YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, Tidal, Amazon Music** | Song names or links only, never who asked | To find and play music |
| **LRCLIB, Radio Browser** | Song names; station searches | For lyrics and radio |
| **Cloudflare** | Visits to laltima.com | It carries the website's traffic |
| **Google Fonts, unpkg.com** | Your browser's request for fonts and the emoji picker | To display the website |
| **Patreon** | Only what you give them, if you click *Support Liora*. Patreon tells Liora nothing: if you link your Discord account there, Patreon gives you the Supporter role in The Agora, and Liora only looks at that role | Tips, and the supporters' thank-you theme |

Each of these has its own privacy policy.

**Inside a server,** some things are shown to that server's staff so they can keep it safe: reports Liora passes
on, who sent a confession, who joined with which invite, and the moderation logs they switch on. **Liora's team**
(Bliss and Mother (Veronica), and nobody else) can open and change any server's dashboard, and every change they make is labelled in
that server's security log. See [Invites & team visits](https://laltima.com/policies/16-invites-and-team-visits) and
[The dashboard](https://laltima.com/policies/17-dashboard-and-website).

## 4. The website and logging in

You log in to laltima.com **with Discord**. Liora only asks Discord who you are and which servers you're in, never
your email. Your login stays in **your browser** (a secure cookie, up to 30 days), not on Liora's side, and
logging out cancels it with Discord too. The site remembers small things like your theme in your own browser. No
ads, no trackers. Details: [The dashboard & laltima.com](https://laltima.com/policies/17-dashboard-and-website).

## 5. Keeping it safe

- Liora's data is stored on the computer that runs her, looked after by Bliss. Liora's team and her users are all
  over the world, and the outside services in section 3 (like Discord and Google) handle information in their
  own countries, mostly the United States. Wherever you are, the promises in this policy stay the same.
- Her conversation memory and her impressions of people are **encrypted**, and so are her backups.
- The secret keys that protect Liora (her Discord token, the website's keys) are never shared or published.
- The website only works over secure HTTPS connections.

No system is perfect. If something ever went wrong with your information, we'd tell the servers affected in The
Agora and through Liora.

## 6. What stays when you ask to be forgotten

`/privacy forgetme` erases almost everything. A few things stay, on purpose:

- **Moderation records** (strikes, warnings, reports about you). Letting people erase their own warnings would
  defeat the point. They follow their own time limits instead (strikes fade after 6 months, entries go after 2
  years).
- **An adults-only server's closed door** (you picked Under 18 there). It's a safety record, like a moderation
  note, so it stays for its year; the server's staff can clear it early. See
  [Age & safety](https://laltima.com/policies/00-age-and-safety).
- **Messages in a server.** Anything posted in a server (logs, confessions, quote cards, transcripts) is an
  ordinary Discord message, and that server's staff decide what to keep. Liora can't delete other people's
  servers' messages for you.
- **Safeguard's evidence cases and the safety watchlist.** They exist so threats and doxxing can be reported and
  dangerous accounts recognised, so the person they're about can't erase them. They follow their own limits instead.
  See [Safeguard](https://laltima.com/policies/19-safeguard).
- **Blacklists and One-Way Door lists.** Like strikes, they're a server's moderation records (and Liora's blocklist is
  Bliss's), so they stay until the people who keep them take you off. Being unbanned also takes you off a server's
  One-Way Door list. See [Moderation](https://laltima.com/policies/07-moderation-and-safety).
- **Server lore** mentioning you. The server's staff can remove it with `/lore delete`.
- **A fingerprint for gifts you've had.** The Agora's welcome and the wedding gift are once per person, ever. If
  you'd had one, Liora keeps a fingerprint: a one-way scramble of your account ID, made with a secret that only
  exists on Liora's own computer. It can't be turned back into your ID and says nothing about you or what you did;
  it only stops the same gift being claimed twice. Nothing else about you is kept.

## 7. Age

Liora is made for **adults (18+)**. Everyone on Discord must be at least 13, following Discord's own rules. If
someone tells Liora they're under 18, she stays strictly wholesome with them everywhere. Server owners decide who
can join their server and are responsible for its age settings. More: [Age & safety](https://laltima.com/policies/00-age-and-safety).

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and let people know in The Agora.
Liora's features and their policy sections are kept in step: when something about your information changes,
the section changes with it.

## 9. Questions and requests

Anything this policy doesn't answer, or anything `/privacy mydata` and `/privacy forgetme` can't do for you, ask
**Bliss** on Discord (**@thenamesbliss**) or in Liora's home server, **The Agora**.
