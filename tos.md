# Liora's Terms of Service

*Last updated: 1 November 2026*

Welcome! These are the ground rules for using Liora Altima, the Discord companion, and her website laltima.com.
By adding Liora to a server, talking with her, or using her features or website, you agree to them. They sit
alongside **Discord's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines**, which always apply too.

Liora is made and run by **Bliss** (BlissfulWizard) with a small team of friends. How she handles your
information is in her [Privacy Policy](https://laltima.com/privacy).

---

## 1. What Liora is

- A friendly AI character who lives in Discord servers, with games, XP, music, moderation tools and more.
- **A hobby project in a small closed beta.** She's looked after with love, but she's still growing: features can
  change, break, pause or be removed, and she may be offline sometimes.
- **Provided as she is.** We do our best, but we can't promise she'll always be available, error-free, or right.

## 2. Who can use her

- You must follow Discord's age rules (at least 13).
- **Liora is made for adults (18+).** She keeps things clean outside age-restricted channels and is strictly
  wholesome with anyone who says they're under 18, but she isn't a babysitter.
- **Server owners** decide who joins their server and which channels are age-restricted, and are responsible for
  those settings, for their staff, and for how they use Liora's tools in their server.

## 3. Be kind: the rules

Using Liora means not doing any of these:

**With Liora herself**
- Trying to trick her into harming people, breaking rules, or saying things she shouldn't ("jailbreaking").
- Trying to get sexual content from her outside age-restricted channels, or **involving minors in anything sexual
  at all**.
- Using her to harass, threaten, expose or bully anyone, including through confessions, quote cards, polls or
  games.
- Sharing other people's private information with her.

**With her features**
- Farming XP, Embers or points with spam, scripts, alt accounts, or idling in voice.
- Using bugs or exploits for gain. **Please report bugs instead**; that's how Liora gets better.
- Rigging votes, or pressuring people with proposals, recruiting or rivalries. An invitation is never pressure,
  and no means no.
- Abusing reports, tickets or Library reports to harass people or flood staff.

**On the website**
- Attacking, overloading or trying to break into laltima.com, or pretending to be someone else, including
  Liora's team.

## 4. What happens if the rules are broken

- **In a server,** its own staff decide, using the server's rules and Liora's moderation tools.
- **With Liora,** we may stop her from talking with you, or block you from her features, in one server or
  everywhere. Rewards earned unfairly may be removed.
- **Serious cases,** like anything sexual involving minors, or threats of real harm, are blocked everywhere and
  reported to Discord.

If you think a decision was unfair, talk to that server's staff first, or to Bliss.

## 5. Embers, XP and other rewards

- XP, levels, Embers, cosmetics, companions, badges and everything like them are **just for fun**. They have **no
  real-world value**, can't be exchanged for money, and aren't your property.
- **They can never be bought with real money,** and Embers can't be sent between people. If Liora ever offers paid
  extras, they will only ever be cosmetic.
- Servers can reset their own XP, and rewards can change as Liora grows.

## 6. Supporting Liora

**Safety never costs a thing.** Everything Liora does to keep a server and its people safe (her Shield against raids,
scams and hacked accounts, Gatekeeper and verification, Safeguard, moderation, logs and backups) is free for every
server, and always will be. Nothing that keeps people safe will ever be put behind a payment, a Patreon tier or a
supporter perk.

Tips through **Patreon** are completely optional and never buy an advantage: no Embers, no XP, no pay-to-win.
Supporters get our thanks and small thank-you gestures, like the Supporter role in The Agora and the Hearthkeeper theme
on the dashboard. They change how things look, never what you can do. Patreon's own terms apply to anything you give there.

## 7. AI replies

Liora is an AI. She can be wrong, mix things up, or misunderstand. **What she says isn't professional advice**
(medical, legal, money or anything else). If you're struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or to
local support services.

## 8. Liora's team

Bliss and Mother (Veronica), and nobody else, look after her across every server: they can visit servers she lives
in and open any server's dashboard to help and fix things. This is always visible in the server's audit and
security logs. See [Invites & team visits](https://laltima.com/policies/16-invites-and-team-visits) and
[The dashboard](https://laltima.com/policies/17-dashboard-and-website).

## 9. Ending things

- **Server owners** can remove Liora at any time (and use the dashboard's Leave Server button).
- **You** can stop using her whenever you like, and `/privacy forgetme` erases what she keeps about you.
- **We** may stop Liora from working in a server that breaks these terms or Discord's rules, or end the project
  entirely. If Liora ever shuts down for good, we'll say so in The Agora first where we can.

## 10. Responsibility

Liora is a free hobby project provided **as she is**. As far as the law allows, Bliss and Liora's team aren't
responsible for losses caused by using her or by her being unavailable, including lost XP, Embers, settings or
messages. Server owners are responsible for their own servers and how they set Liora up.

## 11. Changes

We may update these terms as Liora grows. If something important changes, we'll update the date at the top and
let people know in The Agora. Using Liora after a change means you accept the new terms.

## 12. Questions

Ask **Bliss** on Discord (**@thenamesbliss**) or in Liora's home server, **The Agora**.
